# Users

What each user needs from Vantage, and the product behavior that answers it. These come from the
owner's conversations with junior Marines and section leaders. They are product inputs, not research
findings.

| User | What they need | What Vantage does |
|---|---|---|
| Junior Marine (analyst) | Know what is theirs and what to do next; stop retyping; get credit that holds up | Today lists held work with the next procedure step; claiming puts work on their list at once; every entry lands in their Record attributed to them; Quick Capture for everything else |
| NCO | Work cases and help others; see shared work without a roll call | Handoff with a note; the queue shows who holds what; NCO role sees section totals |
| SNCO / SNCOIC / Team leader | See workload, blockers, waiting, aging, and who carries what; assign work | Team → Workload and the leader's Today: unassigned, overdue, blocked, waiting by category, per-person counts with definitions; handoff and assignment |
| Officer (3404) / section OIC | Understand section throughput and bottlenecks without judging individuals | Section totals count distinct documents; limitations stated on the page |
| Reviewer | Review decisions and verifications with their evidence | Every decision carries a rationale, every verification a reference; history shows who, when and where each value came from. A dedicated reviewer role is not yet built (ROADMAP). |
| Command leadership | Evidence that the tool is used and the work moves | Section workload and resolved/verified counts; the usage console reports adoption without naming people |
| Administrator (unit) | Manage members, roles and units without reading private records | Team → Roster, Roles, Units, Invitations; private entries, drafts and career plans never appear |
| Operator (instance) | Keep the instance healthy, backed up and configured | Owner console: settings, accounts, audit chain, backup, governance, usage; no read access to private records |

## What junior Marines reported, and the response

- **Research eats the day.** The procedure panel shows the next step and what the value means, with
  detail behind "How to do this". Entries are typed once and reused in calculations and drafts.
- **Repetitive entry is frustrating.** The UMT amount comes from the imported sheet. The calculation reads
  recorded values. Drafts assemble facts already recorded.
- **Missing or inconsistent SOPs cause errors.** Procedures are versioned and state their authority and
  limits. Unanswered questions are written down (`docs/domain/SME_QUESTIONS.md`), not guessed.
- **Spreadsheet taskers should become claimable work.** Import → queue → claim → record.
- **Contributions must stay attributed.** The case history keeps each person's entries through
  handoffs and stage changes.
- **Career, JEPES/FITREP, education and certifications matter.** Career plan and next steps, Readiness,
  training, awards, counseling, Reports.
- **A slow tool will not be used.** Quick Capture from Today and `N` anywhere; one form per step.
