# Vision

**Do the work once. Keep the record. Know what comes next.**

Vantage is a Marine Corps work, accountability, performance-record and career-development platform.
A Marine finds or receives work, claims it, does it, and keeps an accurate record of their part in it
without rebuilding that history in another tracker. A leader sees actual workload, progress, blockers
and waiting without collecting updates by hand. The same Marine records what happens outside a
tasker, keeps goals, plans their development, and prepares supported evaluation input.

These are one product. The case a Marine researches today is the fact in their record tomorrow and
the evidence in their evaluation input next quarter. None of it is typed twice.

## What Vantage is not

- Not a system of record. DAI, Advana, GCSS-MC, MOL and the other authoritative systems remain
  authoritative. Vantage organizes the human work around them, calculates transparently, keeps the
  evidence, and says where every value came from.
- Not a scoring or surveillance tool. It never labels people and never estimates effort from typing
  or open tabs. A count always comes with its definition, its window, and what it cannot show.
- Not dependent on a commercial host, a public CDN, an AI service or a personal account.

## Who it is for first

Junior Marines, NCOs and SNCOs, and the leaders of a budget-execution section: 3451 analysts and 3404
officers, likely in a MARFORRES or G-8 pilot. Financial management is the first specialization modeled
in depth. The work, record, goals and career surfaces serve any MOS.

## What success looks like in a pilot

- A new Marine finds their assigned work, claims an item, knows the next step, and finds their own
  contribution without coaching.
- Leaders stop running spreadsheet roll calls for status.
- Marines use the Record when preparing JEPES or FITREP input because it is already built.
- An infrastructure team can install, operate, back up and evaluate it on approved servers.
