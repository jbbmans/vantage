# VANTAGE video storyboards

15 September 2026. Source build `659cbba`.

Production storyboards; replacement footage, final voice and music not yet produced or approved.

## Creative and production standard

Every film opens with an outcome, demonstrates a complete task, and closes with a useful next step. Use current real UI and fictional accounts in a disposable environment. Existing clips are reference material, not approved masters.

Voice: warm, assured, conversational adult voice; neutral US English; 135-150 words/minute while speaking, with purposeful silent reading holds. No military announcer delivery. Pronounce VANTAGE “VAN-tij”, USMC letter by letter, UMT and ULO letter by letter, JEPES “JEP-ess”, FITREP “FIT-rep”, DAI letter by letter. Read dollar amounts in full. Cast a human or natural licensed synthetic voice; never clone an identifiable person without permission.

Music: licensed instrumental or an original composition with recorded provenance. Follow the per-film tempo and mood below. No chart-song references, vocals, relentless risers or trailer booms. Mix voice ahead of music. Target the stereo master around -16 LUFS integrated and <= -1 dBTP; use a low music bed during speech, typically 18-24 dB lower than narration, adjusted by listening. Test mono, phone speaker and headphones. These are production targets, not legal broadcast standards.

Editing: 1920x1080 delivery, 30 fps; capture UI at 2560x1440 or higher before reframing. Use 200-350ms eased punch-ins, 110-140% crops only while text stays sharp, match cuts on identical source records and J/L cuts connecting explanation to action. Speed up typing or loading only, visibly labeling meaningful elapsed-time compression. Keep save/confirmation and permission transitions understandable. Do not invent UI through AI video.

Captions: accurate human-reviewed WebVTT and SRT, synchronized to final audio within 100ms; two lines maximum, roughly 42 characters/line; verify reading speed. Use a separate social cut with open captions. Keep captions in a reserved safe area; never obscure controls, status, amount or evidence. No autoplay audio on the homepage.

Delivery: H.264/AAC MP4 with faststart, optional VP9/Opus WebM, poster, WebVTT, SRT, transcript, chapter list, clean master and captioned mobile reframe. Derive duration and publication metadata from the final files. Do not mark a placeholder or unapproved film as published.

Release gate: complete demonstrated journey; independent role checks where relevant; exact source/amount/date agreement; final spoken audio reviewed; music rights recorded; no credentials or actual personnel data; current flat branding; readable mobile captions; no clip starts with setup or blank loading; no cut implies an action happened when it did not. Every storyboard is a proposed recording plan; behavior must be verified before its narration is used.
