# Vantage: command pilot, adoption and personal benefit packet

Prepared for John Boletz. Planning guidance and draft review material, not a government endorsement, legal ownership determination, procurement authorization or promise of an award.

## What to call it

"Vantage is a Marine-developed workspace that turns financial case information into assigned, documented and verified action. It brings the source, research, calculation, handoff and history together. We are seeking a sponsored, measured pilot on an approved environment."

Describe a candidate calculation as a candidate. Vantage does not post an obligation, approve a funds check or clear an error in DAI merely because a case is closed in Vantage. Avoid claiming MCEN approval, enterprise adoption, artificial-intelligence necessity or realized dollar savings without evidence.

## The concrete pilot request

Ask the chain of command for a named sponsor, financial process owner and G-6/security point of contact to evaluate a small pilot. Propose five analysts and two reviewers in one unit, using synthetic data until the host and data owner authorize otherwise. The numbers below are proposed targets, not measured results.

| Period | Activity | Evidence to keep |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Observe the current process on representative ULO/UMT cases. Agree what counts as a completed, verified action. | Task time, rework, duplicate handling, lost handoffs and training time; no unnecessary personal data. |
| Week 2 | Train the small pilot group; rehearse import, assignment, research, verification, reporting and restore. | A short task checklist, observed misunderstandings and a verified recovery exercise. |
| Weeks 3–4 | Run the approved pilot with a reachable support owner and daily issue triage. | Median task time, reviewed error rate, blocked work age, verified completions and repeat usage. |
| Week 5 | Have a reviewer independently check a sample against authoritative systems and original documents. | Discrepancies, causes and corrections; do not count mere case closure as financial clearance. |
| Week 6 | Brief results and decide whether to stop, improve, or expand to another unit. | Before/after comparison, support cost, open defects, data-owner decision and sponsor statement. |

Suggested acceptance criteria: no unauthorized disclosure, no duplicate credited outcome, no unreconciled data loss, successful restore, no unresolved severe defect, and a meaningful measured improvement agreed before the pilot. Set numerical time/error targets after the baseline exists. A failed authority or integrity check stops the affected workflow until investigated.

Use person-hours saved = verified time saved per comparable case × comparable verified cases / 60. Separate labor capacity from cash savings. Released obligation value, corrected transaction value and dollars saved are different measures and must not be added together as one benefit. Include implementation, hosting, training, support and review time in any cost comparison.

## A demonstration that fits a staff meeting

1. State one familiar problem: the spreadsheet lists a document but does not establish who owns the next action or what proves it cleared.
2. Import a synthetic report, inspect the mapping/preview and show that reimport does not create duplicate work.
3. Preview assignment of selected cases and show one stale/unauthorized refusal being handled explicitly.
4. Open one case. Show the source, a research observation, exact-money calculation and the rule requiring review/verification.
5. Hand it off with context; show the history and the new holder's list.
6. Show a leadership report and explain what its totals do and do not establish. Close with the specific pilot request, support owner and next decision date.

Bring a one-page problem/benefit brief, the synthetic demo, test/release evidence, an architecture/data-flow summary, the pilot checklist and the current limitations. Route external/public demonstrations through the command's release process. Do not present operational document numbers, account details or personnel records on a public site or in a public repository.

## How to build career credit

Maintain a dated record of the problem you identified, the decisions you led, the people you trained and outcomes another leader verified. Keep legitimate authorship/commit history, presentation records, approvals, issue logs and measured results. Request attribution as project originator/product lead and an appointed sponsor/support structure; share credit with reviewers, users and maintainers.

Use accurate evaluation language after results exist: "Led a [number]-Marine pilot; reduced median [defined task] time from [baseline] to [result] across [sample] comparable cases; trained [number] users and established [verified control]." Empty brackets are deliberate. Do not invent metrics or write future success as an accomplished fact.

Ask the chain about an appropriate innovation billet, funded training, a documented project-lead role and recognition based on verified impact. These can create durable career value even when the government has rights in the software. Keep your finance education and MOS performance progressing alongside the project; do not become the only person who can operate it.

## Monetary benefit and ownership: obtain a concrete determination

Preserve the current repository notices and authorship evidence, but do not treat a copyright notice or public GitHub hosting as proof that you exclusively own the project. Under 17 U.S.C. 101/105, official-duty government work has specific copyright treatment; counsel must apply the facts to this project. [Copyright Office, Title 17](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html)

FAR 3.601 generally restricts contracts with government employees and businesses they own/control; 3.602 provides a narrow authorized exception. An LLC, a contractor intermediary or a colleague's name does not establish an approved route. Obtain ethics/acquisition guidance before negotiating a sale or paid support connected to your duties. [FAR 3.601](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/3.601) · [FAR 3.602](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/3.602)

10 U.S.C. 1124 authorizes certain military innovation cash awards, with an aggregate statutory ceiling of $25,000 for an innovation. Eligibility, approval and funding are separate questions, and acceptance carries government-use and title assurances. This is a possible recognized route, not money already earned. [10 U.S.C. 1124](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1124&num=0&edition=prelim)

MARADMIN 411/26's second Innovation Challenge round is oriented to M365 Power Platform and includes broad USMC ownership terms for associated/submitted software. Confirm eligibility and have the terms reviewed before submitting this React/Node project. An innovation competition is not a neutral licensing step. [Official announcement](https://www.marines.mil/News/Messages/Messages-Display/Article/4598975/announcement-of-the-second-round-of-the-usmc-innovation-challenge/)

Prepare this fact sheet for the servicing ethics/legal and acquisition offices:

| Fact to establish | Evidence to gather |
|---|---|
| Your duties and tasking when the idea/code was created | Billet description, written tasking, duty status and dates. |
| Resources used | Government/personal devices, duty/off-duty time, paid tools, hosting payments and assistance. |
| Contributors and preexisting material | Commit history, authors, outside code/assets, existing notices and dependency licenses. |
| Existing promises and submissions | Emails, agreements, competition terms, disclosures, licenses and prior command direction. |
| Intended paid activity | Exact proposed customer, service/license, company/control, pricing role and relationship to your official duties. |

Request written answers on ownership/government rights, outside activity approval, permissible commercialization, conflict/recusal obligations, award eligibility and acceptance terms. Provide actual facts, not a conclusion that you privately own everything or that the government owns everything. Do not transfer rights, remove notices, enroll in a competition, solicit official business or accept binding terms merely to create a clearer story.

The highest-confidence path now is verified operational impact plus documented credit, while written guidance determines whether a lawful commercial path exists. Monetary success is uncertain; career value is strengthened by a useful, maintainable product and independently confirmed results.

## Operational ownership and retirement

Before expansion, assign a product owner, host operator, security/identity owner, records/data owner, backup/restore owner, training lead and support backup. Agree which incident channel is staffed and when. Maintain deployment/rollback instructions, release evidence and an approved data-retention schedule.

If the pilot stops, document why, export only what each person/unit is authorized to take, reconcile open actions in authoritative systems, revoke sessions and integrations, apply holds/retention, and retire the host through its approved process. Deleting a repository or switching off a server is not a complete records disposition.
